Read the payer column correctly. At 2.3× LTV:CAC and a 15-month payback, payer contracts do not pay for themselves as a standalone product, and we are not pretending otherwise. The payer contract is a distribution channel: signing one payer makes Pavo reachable by every provider group already submitting authorizations to it, which is why provider CAC falls 37% across the plan. Judged alone the payer line looks weak; judged as the thing that makes the provider line work, it is the highest-leverage spend in the model.
